# Requirements Engineering Workflow

## Steps
1. Identify business problem, stakeholders, actors, and measurable outcomes.
2. Capture current process and pain points; distinguish facts from assumptions.
3. Define scope, exclusions, constraints, dependencies, and data sensitivity.
4. Write atomic, uniquely identified functional requirements.
5. Define measurable non-functional requirements with measurement method and environment.
6. Map business rules, use cases, alternate flows, exceptions, and permissions.
7. Define acceptance criteria in observable Given/When/Then or equivalent form.
8. Review feasibility, conflicts, duplication, ambiguity, and testability.
9. Obtain business/product owner approval for requirements and unresolved policy decisions.
10. Maintain traceability to modules, API, data, tests, and releases.

## Outputs
PRD, FR, NFR, business process, use cases, acceptance criteria, traceability matrix, and decision log.

## Exit criteria
Scope and owners are clear; high-priority requirements are testable; assumptions/open decisions are explicit; approval is recorded.

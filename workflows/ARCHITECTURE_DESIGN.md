# Architecture Design Workflow

## Steps
1. Confirm approved requirements, constraints, and quality attributes.
2. Inspect existing architecture and implementation.
3. Identify boundaries, components, data ownership, integrations, trust boundaries, and failure modes.
4. Compare viable options using cost, complexity, security, operability, and maintainability.
5. Define API, database, authorization, queue/cache/storage, and deployment implications where relevant.
6. Record material choices in ADRs.
7. Review compatibility, migration, observability, and recovery.
8. Validate design against requirements and implementation constraints.
9. Update architecture diagrams and documentation after approval.

## Outputs
System/component/integration views, ADRs, data/API designs, risks, and implementation plan.

## Exit criteria
Responsibilities and interfaces are clear; key trade-offs and risks are documented; required approvals obtained.

# Definition of Ready

A task is Ready when applicable items are satisfied:

- [ ] Business/user problem and desired outcome are clear.
- [ ] Scope and exclusions are stated.
- [ ] Relevant actors, roles, and permissions are identified.
- [ ] Acceptance criteria are observable and testable.
- [ ] Dependencies and constraints are known or explicitly tracked.
- [ ] Data/API/schema impact is understood at an appropriate level.
- [ ] Security, privacy, and operational risks are considered.
- [ ] Design/ADR approval exists where required.
- [ ] Test approach and environment are feasible.
- [ ] Owner and priority are assigned.

Do not block a small task on irrelevant paperwork. Record unresolved assumptions and proceed only when safe.
